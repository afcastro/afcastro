## What I do

Reproducible empirical work on macroeconomic and monetary questions, with a post-Keynesian and Global South lens: time-series econometrics (VAR/VECM, Johansen cointegration), exchange-rate and external-constraint analysis, and payment-systems research (PIX, Drex, monetary sovereignty).
